# Brief for sales on project p_1778324230_fd9650

Build an institutional buyer database for real estate wholesale. Target: REITs, iBuyers (Opendoor, Offerpad, etc.), hedge funds, private equity, and large cash buyer networks. For each buyer type: 1) Their typical buy box criteria (price range, property condition, geography, volume per month). 2) How to contact their acquisition teams. 3) Their preferred deal structure (assignment vs double-close). 4) Typical assignment fee margins they allow. Return a buyer matrix prioritized by Texas and surrounding states, with contact strategy.
